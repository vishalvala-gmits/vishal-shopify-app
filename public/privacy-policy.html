Privacy Policy

App: Jewelry Savings Scheme

Developer: Iqonic

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Contact: inquiry@iqonic.tech

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Jewelry Savings Scheme ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when a merchant installs the App on their Shopify store, and when a store's customers use the savings calculator on the storefront.

The App lets a merchant configure a monthly savings plan (contribution range, duration, a bonus month, and optional free gifts). Customers can use a calculator on the storefront to see an estimated payout, and may submit an enquiry with their contact details to register interest. The App does not process payments, change checkout totals, or modify product prices in the store's catalog.

2. Information We Collect

Merchant configuration data

When a merchant sets up the App, they enter scheme configuration such as contribution amounts, plan duration, bonus terms, gift details, and display settings. This configuration is stored in the App's own database, associated with the merchant's store. It is used only to run the App for that store.

Customer enquiry data

When a customer uses the calculator and chooses to submit an enquiry, we collect the information they provide: name, and phone number and/or email address. We also store the monthly amount they selected and the calculated plan totals at the time of submission, so the merchant can follow up with an accurate quote. This information is stored in the App's database and is visible only to the merchant who owns the store, through the App's admin dashboard.

Customer enquiry data is not linked to a Shopify customer account, used for marketing, or shared with any third party.

Shopify API access

The App requests only the access scopes needed to read basic product/shop information and to display the calculator via a theme app block. It does not request access to order data or payment details beyond what Shopify requires for basic app functionality.

Basic technical data

Like most web applications, our systems may automatically log limited technical information (such as request timestamps or error logs) for the purpose of maintaining and troubleshooting the App. We do not use this information for tracking or profiling.

3. How We Use Information

Any information described above is used solely to:

  • Operate and display the calculator according to the merchant's configured scheme
  • Let the merchant view and follow up on customer enquiries submitted through their own store
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the App
  • Respond to merchant support requests

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share merchant or customer information with third parties. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services within the App.

5. Data Retention

Merchant configuration data and customer enquiries persist for as long as the App remains installed on the store, or until the merchant deletes a specific enquiry from the App's admin dashboard. If a merchant uninstalls the App, all associated scheme configuration and customer enquiry data is deleted from our database within Shopify's standard 48-hour uninstall window.

6. Data Security

Customer enquiry and merchant configuration data is stored in a database that is not publicly accessible and is only exposed to the merchant through their own authenticated Shopify admin session. We do not maintain any public-facing API that returns this data.

7. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Other Applicable Laws)

Customers who have submitted an enquiry through a merchant's store, and who wish to access, correct, or delete their information, should first contact the merchant, since the merchant is the data controller for enquiries submitted on their store. Merchants can view and delete individual enquiries directly from the App's admin dashboard at any time.

For any request that cannot be resolved with the merchant directly, or for questions about how the App itself handles data, contact us at inquiry@iqonic.tech.

8. Shopify Mandatory Compliance Webhooks

As required by Shopify for all apps listed on the Shopify App Store, we support the following mandatory GDPR webhooks:

  • customers/data_request — acknowledged by the App; enquiry records are not linked to a Shopify customer ID, so any matching data is identified and handled via the merchant's own enquiry records.
  • customers/redact — when triggered, the App deletes any stored enquiries matching the customer's email or phone number for that store.
  • shop/redact — when a store uninstalls the App and the retention period elapses, all scheme configuration and customer enquiry data associated with that store is permanently deleted.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by Shopify merchants and their storefront customers in a general retail context. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
